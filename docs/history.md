# History

Where the kit and its rules came from: the tutorial and sales videos of
Plenoview (`github.com/magicmiguelx/plenoview`, a browser app for drawing
multi-platform data architectures in 3D), recorded in October 2026. The
commits are in that repository; the kit was extracted from its
`tests/e2e/demo.ts`, `tests/e2e/demos.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/promo.spec.ts`,
`scripts/voice.mjs` and `scripts/voiceover.mjs` at commit `4f81cdf`
(2026-10-11), made app-agnostic, and given a heartbeat for pages that
draw nothing between actions.

| 2026-10-12 | Plenoview re-recorded under the speed limit, the card flat | The two rules ported to the app's copies and the whole series (23 tutorials, 7 promos) re-recorded. `voiceover speak` first: with the cache empty every clip was made under the ceiling in one pass, and only 5 of 293 lines could not fit at the slowest speed (3.0 to 3.6 words a second); each was fixed by a word, since a written pause ("...", `<break>`) was measured not to lengthen a clip. Two recordings in parallel on four cores starved each other: a tutorial came out 28 s longer and its closing card a second ahead of its voice (the screencast frame's content lags its timestamp by a whole frame interval, a second under contention); recorded one at a time, both ends landed within 0.2 s. Porting it taught three things, each a re-recording of the series: a CSS-animated dot gives stale frames while the main thread is busy (a timer now steps it), a card hides a dot below it (it sits above), and with a heartbeat on, no frames means the page cannot paint, so `painted()` waits longer and counts the fade from the first frame after the change (kit and copy alike). Vite's dependency optimizer reloaded the page at the first tour-video export (`mp4-muxer` discovered mid-take): every lazily imported package now in `optimizeDeps.include`. The silences the script tables still showed (nine to seventeen seconds) were a mocked answer waited for in full, a goal typed twice, the app's own tour recorder, and one line over six clicks. |

| 2026-10-14 | What a line is about is in the picture | The owner saw the focus of a line cut off at the bottom of the frame. A frame sheet at every line of PF1e Campaigner's series found about twenty: lines said in full before a click on a button below the fold, sections whose header was the last thing on screen, lines said before what they described had opened. The driver measures each line's subject in the picture (the elements it names with `about`, the spotlit one, the one its first action works on, before the action scrolls), hit-tested so a sticky header hides; `spot()` and clicks bring their element to the middle; the script gains a "Seen" column, `minInView` fails a take, `screenplay-voiceover seen` lists the series. | 2026-10-15 | `screenplay-voiceover` refused every command | Since the first commit a flag left out (`--config`, later `--over`, `--under`) dropped the argument at index 0, the command, so `voiceover check 07` printed the usage; it only ran with every flag given. The flags are parsed by `node:util`'s `parseArgs`, a share out of 0 to 1 is refused, and `tests/unit/voiceover.test.mjs` runs the built CLI from a project root, as a project does. |
| 2026-10-15 | A failed take left its frames in `/tmp` | The frames folder was removed only by `save()`; the smoke test's deliberately failed take left 417 JPEGs, and a real full-HD take hundreds of megabytes. The recorder removes it when its page closes and when the process exits too (`dispose()`), and the smoke test asserts both. |
| 2026-10-15 | The voice tooling, tightened | The clip cache keyed on the voice, the model and the text, not the settings: a change of stability or speed got the old clips. Settings off the defaults are in the key now (on the defaults the key is unchanged, so no cache is lost). `check` compared bags of words, so a word added or heard once of two passed: it lines the transcript up in order, lists words missing and words added, leaves sound tags out, and exits 1 like `cover` and `seen`. `transcribe()` retries a busy or rate-limited service like every other call, its model is `voice.transcriber`. The mix's padding (a second encode of the whole video when the last clip outlasts the picture) was measured with a stand-in voice service on localhost: a voiced take never needs it, since `save()` waits for the last line; only `voiceover mix` over a video recorded with shorter lengths than its clips does, and it now says so, and a script with no lines is left alone. `speakable()` lost its Plenoview phrasings ("with +,", "with ×") for general rules (a bare "+" or "×"), and the voice lookup its "professional" guess for an exact name or `voiceId`. |
| 2026-10-15 | The Seen bookkeeping made pure | The rules for which line a subject's measurement belongs to (a line said in full, then acted on; a spotlight held for the next line; a cut or a card ending the wait) lived in ten fields of the 700-line driver, tested only by the smoke recording. They are `src/subjects.ts` now, unit-tested like the timeline; `say()` is `note()` plus the hold, and `card()` and `cardAcross()` share how a card goes up, so a fix to one is a fix to both. |
| 2026-10-15 | A caption's corners; every rushed line in one take | The smoke test's tall section had its caption over 100% of content at the bottom and the top: with two positions, a page busy across its middle had nowhere to put one. The corners are measured too and a clearly emptier one is taken, only when both centred positions are over `coverNote` (or `maxCover`), so a page with room looks as before (`corners: false` to keep them centred). A voiced take stopped at its first line over the speed limit, one take per rewrite; it now gives the line its room at the ceiling, goes on, and fails at `save()` with every such line, as `voiceover speak` does over finished scripts. Both tested in the smoke run, the second against a stand-in voice service on localhost (no key, no bill), which also covers the voiced `save()` and mix for the first time. |
| 2026-10-15 | CI, and a version to pin | The kit had no CI and one version, 0.1.0, while projects installed `main`: any push changed every project's next install. A workflow runs the typecheck, the unit tests and the smoke recording on every push (no key; the voiced part against the stand-in service); releases are tagged from 0.2.0, and `docs/adopting.md` has projects pin one. |
| 2026-10-16 | The default voice named a voice that is not there | Re-recording Plenoview on 0.2.0 failed at its first line: no voice named "James - Professional Male" in the account or the library. The series' voice is the library's "James - Professional British Male"; the default had only ever worked through the lookup's "professional" guess, removed in 0.2.0, and would have failed on a fresh account either way. | 2026-10-16 | Corners and a canvas | Plenoview's first take on 0.2.0 put five of six captions in corners, one on the Layers panel: the 3D scene's canvas fills the frame and counts as content wherever a caption sits, so both centred positions read as busy and a corner won by covering a panel. The playbook and the adopting guide say an app drawn on a full-frame canvas keeps `corners: false`, as Plenoview now does. |
| 2026-10-16 | A smooth scroll that started late | Two of Plenoview's tutorials hung thirty minutes each on 0.2.0: a click on a palette item far down its panel landed at y 2055 of an 810-pixel frame. `bringIntoView` asked for a smooth scroll and took two equal readings 100 ms apart, before the busy page had begun it, as the end of it; a diagnostic spec showed the item at 1793 when measured and at 588 a moment later. It now waits for the element to be in the frame and still (three seconds, then an instant scroll), and `center()` fails at once when the middle of what it acts on is outside the frame. The smoke test delays `scrollIntoView` by 400 ms to reproduce it; with the old wait it fails exactly so. |
| 2026-10-16 | A clipped menu item read as seen | Plenoview's tutorial 8 then failed at File → Custom node types: the item sat in the frame but below the bottom of the menu's own scrolling list, the point at its middle was the 3D scene, and the click there closed the menu. The first fix had waited for the element to be "in the frame", which a clipped item already was. The wait now asks for the element to have moved or to be clipped by nothing; and the in-view share no longer counts a hit on any box the element sits in (the page's body included) where a clipping box between them cuts it off, so a clipped item is 0% in view, not 100%, in the Seen column too. The smoke test's menu reproduces it. |
| 2026-10-16 | Plenotrack's 26 videos ported to the kit | Recorded on 8 October by a recorder of the app's own (Playwright's VP8 video, the voice at each scene's wall-clock start, the narration as the only caption), and a day before the app's Help screen began opening at every sign-in. The catalogue was kept and played through the driver by a small adapter; every line got a short caption with the narration as its voice. | 2026-10-16 | The voice's speed limit gave up early; a "..." before "and" | Two of Plenoview's lines failed the ceiling at 3.03 and 3.02 words a second "at speed 0.77" and "0.74": the clip was made at most three times, so the slowest speed, 0.7, was never tried, though the message said it had been. `nextSpeed` (pure, unit-tested) keeps slowing to 0.7, six clips at most, and the message says which limit stopped it.
