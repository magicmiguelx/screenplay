# History

Where the kit and its rules came from: the tutorial and sales videos of
Plenoview (`github.com/magicmiguelx/plenoview`, a browser app for drawing
multi-platform data architectures in 3D), recorded in October 2026. The
commits are in that repository; the kit was extracted from its
`tests/e2e/demo.ts`, `tests/e2e/demos.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/promo.spec.ts`,
`scripts/voice.mjs` and `scripts/voiceover.mjs` at commit `4f81cdf`
(2026-10-11), made app-agnostic, and given a heartbeat for pages that
draw nothing between actions.

| 2026-10-12 | Plenoview re-recorded under the speed limit, the card flat | The two rules ported to the app's copies and the whole series (23 tutorials, 7 promos) re-recorded. `voiceover speak` first: with the cache empty every clip was made under the ceiling in one pass, and only 5 of 293 lines could not fit at the slowest speed (3.0 to 3.6 words a second); each was fixed by a word, since a written pause ("...", `<break>`) was measured not to lengthen a clip. Two recordings in parallel on four cores starved each other: a tutorial came out 28 s longer and its closing card a second ahead of its voice (the screencast frame's content lags its timestamp by a whole frame interval, a second under contention); recorded one at a time, both ends landed within 0.2 s. The cards were measured with per-frame luminance differences (`templates/check-cards.mjs`), because scene detection sees no cut in a half-second fade; the measurement found the keyboard tutorial's closing card 1.3 s behind its voice, since the table view sends no frames and the app's copy lacked the heartbeat. Porting it taught three things, each a re-recording of the series: a CSS-animated dot gives stale frames while the main thread is busy (a timer now steps it), a card hides a dot below it (it sits above), and with a heartbeat on, no frames means the page cannot paint, so `painted()` waits longer and counts the fade from the first frame after the change (kit and copy alike). Vite's dependency optimizer reloaded the page at the first tour-video export (`mp4-muxer` discovered mid-take): every lazily imported package now in `optimizeDeps.include`. The silences the script tables still showed (nine to seventeen seconds) were a mocked answer waited for in full, a goal typed twice, the app's own tour recorder, and one line over six clicks. |

| 2026-10-14 | What a line is about is in the picture | The owner saw the focus of a line cut off at the bottom of the frame. A frame sheet at every line of PF1e Campaigner's series found about twenty: lines said in full before a click on a button below the fold, sections whose header was the last thing on screen, lines said before what they described had opened. The driver measures each line's subject in the picture (the elements it names with `about`, the spotlit one, the one its first action works on, before the action scrolls), hit-tested so a sticky header hides; `spot()` and clicks bring their element to the middle; the script gains a "Seen" column, `minInView` fails a take, `screenplay-voiceover seen` lists the series. A recorder change: every video re-recorded. |
| 2026-10-15 | `screenplay-voiceover` refused every command | Since the first commit a flag left out (`--config`, later `--over`, `--under`) dropped the argument at index 0, the command, so `voiceover check 07` printed the usage; it only ran with every flag given. The flags are parsed by `node:util`'s `parseArgs`, a share out of 0 to 1 is refused, and `tests/unit/voiceover.test.mjs` runs the built CLI from a project root, as a project does. |
| 2026-10-15 | A failed take left its frames in `/tmp` | The frames folder was removed only by `save()`; the smoke test's deliberately failed take left 417 JPEGs, and a real full-HD take hundreds of megabytes. The recorder removes it when its page closes and when the process exits too (`dispose()`), and the smoke test asserts both. |
| 2026-10-15 | The voice tooling, tightened | The clip cache keyed on the voice, the model and the text, not the settings: a change of stability or speed got the old clips. Settings off the defaults are in the key now (on the defaults the key is unchanged, so no cache is lost). `check` compared bags of words, so a word added or heard once of two passed: it lines the transcript up in order, lists words missing and words added, leaves sound tags out, and exits 1 like `cover` and `seen`. `transcribe()` retries a busy or rate-limited service like every other call, its model is `voice.transcriber`. Demo.save() holds the last frame for the last clip in its one encode, so the mix no longer re-encodes the video to pad it. `speakable()` lost its Plenoview phrasings ("with +,", "with ×") for general rules (a bare "+" or "×"), and the voice lookup its "professional" guess for an exact name or `voiceId`. |
